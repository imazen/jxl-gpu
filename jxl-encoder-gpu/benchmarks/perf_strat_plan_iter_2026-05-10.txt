Follow-up investigation (2026-05-10)
------------------------------------
Where the 5.92 ms comes from: encode_with_strategy_plan_adaptive_traced
ends with three `enc.download_plane(&rgb_*)` calls on the GPU-resident
linear RGB planes. `download_plane` is a `read_one` — a blocking sync
that drains the entire CUDA queue. PCIe transfer of 3×4 MB at ~20 GB/s
is ~0.6 ms; the rest (~5.3 ms) is the queue-drain sync stall.

Why the round-trip on every iter is wasteful: callers in
`butteraugli_loop.rs` (e.g. `refine_aq_field_gpu_with_strategy_search`)
take the linear RGB → host-side `linear_planar_to_srgb_u8_interleaved` →
`bg.compute_with_reference(srgb_u8)` which uploads to GPU again.
Net effect: ~12 MB download + host conversion + 3 MB upload per iter.

Two paths to fix (cross-repo dependency):
  (a) Add `populate_linear_from_planar_gpu(target, GpuPlane×3)` to
      butteraugli-gpu's pipeline, plus `set_distorted_from_planar_gpu`.
      Skips both the download AND the host conversion AND the re-upload.
      Estimated saving: ~5.5 ms/iter (33% per-iter wall-clock).
  (b) Add `populate_opsin_from_xyb_gpu` (XYB ≈ opsin in libjxl).
      Bigger saving since butteraugli's preprocessing skips
      sRGB→linear→opsin entirely. We already have XYB on GPU before
      `xyb_to_linear_rgb_planar_persistent`.

Both paths require touching `~/work/zen/zenmetrics/crates/butteraugli-gpu/`,
which the global rules forbid without user authorization.

Within-repo intermediate win (does NOT need cross-repo work):
  Move `linear_planar_to_srgb_u8_interleaved` to GPU + change
  `download_plane` × 3 to a single u8 sRGB download. Cuts PCIe
  bytes 4× (12 MB → 3 MB) but only saves the ~0.4 ms transfer
  portion — the queue-drain stall remains. ~7% win, not dramatic.

Decision: park this until cross-repo authorization. The
benchmark still serves as a regression gate.
